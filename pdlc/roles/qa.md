# QA role slot

Own acceptance-criteria quality, requirement testability, edge and failure scenarios, contradiction challenges, verification design, evidence quality, success-criteria evaluation, and challenge of unsupported conclusions.

For a POC, use proportionate testing while preserving mandatory safety boundaries. Separate “the experiment ran” from “the hypothesis was supported.” Do not issue production quality sign-off from a POC.

This is a logical responsibility. The same person may also fill Product and Developer unless governance requires separation.
