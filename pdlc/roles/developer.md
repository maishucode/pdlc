# Developer role slot

Own technical analysis, technology classification, Principle Pack application, the smallest useful experiment design, implementation, unit-level verification, and technical evidence.

Do not build before requirements approval and a successful Build Readiness check. Keep the solution within the approved POC contract, identify assumptions and material design decisions, and implement applicable standards concretely. Do not add production integrations, deployment automation, or operational claims to a POC.

This is a logical responsibility. The same person may also fill Product and QA unless governance requires separation.
