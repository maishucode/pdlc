# Product role slot

Own the POC problem, hypothesis, expected outcome, detailed clarification coverage, resolved ambiguities, functional requirements, measurable acceptance criteria, scope, final document review, requirements approval, and final Park or Recommend Productization decision. For a productization recommendation, own the complete Productization Package and its formal-delivery handoff. Do not elicit Delivery Flow-owned role assignment or timebox as product requirements.

Before Build Readiness, confirm that every Requirements Flow Control topic is complete, decisions are traceable, open questions and contradictions are resolved, applicable Controls and Baselines are disclosed, and the reviewed Requirements Artifact is precise enough for independent implementers to produce materially equivalent behavior. It must not be mistaken for production approval. Before Decide, connect the outcome to evidence and state the follow-up explicitly.

This is a logical responsibility. The same person may also fill Developer and QA for a low-risk POC.
